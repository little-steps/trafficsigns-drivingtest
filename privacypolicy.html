1. Information We Collect

Traffic Signs & Driving Test does not require users to create an account or log in to use the main educational game features.

We do not directly request or require personal information such as your name, phone number, home address, or account password in order to play the game.

The App is an educational tool for learning traffic signs and preparing for driving-test quizzes. Users can browse sign categories, read sign meanings, choose a preferred language, and practice with quizzes.

If you voluntarily contact us by email for support, feedback, or questions, we may receive the information that you choose to include in your message, including your email address. This information is used only for responding to your request.

Traffic Signs & Driving Test provides learning content about road signs and driving-test preparation. Categories may include:

  • Warning signs
  • Prohibition and restriction signs
  • Mandatory signs
  • Priority signs
  • Information signs
  • Direction and destination signs
  • Road services signs
  • Temporary road-work signs
  • Practice tests and quizzes

The App may store certain game preferences or settings locally on your device, depending on the features enabled in the App.

Examples may include sound preferences, game settings, or other basic preferences required for the App to function properly.

Locally stored information is not intentionally uploaded to our servers by Traffic Signs & Driving Test.

Local information remains on your device unless you remove the App, clear its data, or otherwise remove the stored information through your device settings.

3. Learning and Driving-Test Features

Traffic Signs & Driving Test helps users recognize traffic signs, understand their meanings, and prepare for driving-test quizzes.

The App may provide learning and test modes such as:

  • Learn: Browse traffic signs by category and read their meanings.
  • Practice Test: Answer multiple-choice questions for practice.
  • Timed Test: Practice within a time limit, if this feature is available.
  • By Category: Practice signs from a selected category, if available.
  • Mixed Test: Practice questions from different categories, if available.

The App may display quiz results or progress to help users review their practice. Traffic signs and driving rules can vary by country or region; users should consult their local official driving authority for current rules.
